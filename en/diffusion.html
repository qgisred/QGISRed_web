Training Courses

The first QGISRed training course was held at the Universitat Politècnica de València on 18 July 2022, at the 2nd WDSA-CCWI 2022 Joint Congress, taught by Dr. Paloma Batanero Akerman.

"Managing Advanced Hydraulic Models with QGISRed Plugin. From EPANET to Digital Twins"

Future editions of this course and a full course catalogue will be announced here, covering topics from basic urban hydraulics principles to model building, scenario management for master plans, and migration to Digital Twins.

Courses will primarily be online with tutored assistance. In-person courses are also available upon institutional or corporate request.
