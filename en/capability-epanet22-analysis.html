EPANET 2.2 analysis options

Featured capability · PDA vs DDA

From EPANET 2.0 to EPANET 2.2

EPANET 2.2 brings significant advances over version 2.0, including support for pressure-dependent demands (PDA), new water quality options and improved numerical convergence.

QGISRed exposes these new options through its interface, allowing full use of the simulation engine's capabilities without manually editing .inp files.

DDA versus PDA

The classic EPANET model (DDA — Demand Driven Analysis) assumes that demand at each node is always satisfied, regardless of available pressure. This can produce negative pressures in shortage situations that do not correspond to physical reality.

The PDA (Pressure Driven Analysis) model calculates the demand actually served based on the available pressure at the node:

  • If pressure is above the minimum threshold, full demand is served.
  • If pressure falls below the minimum, the served flow is proportionally reduced.
  • If pressure reaches zero, no demand is served.

Integration with Toolkit 2.3

QGISRed uses the EPANET Toolkit dynamic library to run simulations directly from QGIS, without needing to open the EPANET desktop application. QGISRed version 0.17 incorporates Toolkit 2.3, which includes:

  • Performance improvements for large networks
  • Fixes in water quality calculations in PDA mode
  • New reporting and results export options
  • Compatibility with chained simulation scenarios
Note: To use PDA analysis, you must define the Minimum service pressure and Full service pressure parameters in the hydraulic options. These values are configured from the QGISRed simulation options dialogue.
